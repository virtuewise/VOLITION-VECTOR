# Trust, credentials and verification

## Distinct claims

1. Account ownership and authentication.
2. Identity verification.
3. Organization verification.
4. Professional qualification issued by an authoritative body.
5. Demonstrated capability supported by portfolio or work evidence.
6. Platform transaction and collaboration history.

Never collapse these into one generic verified badge.

## Credential lifecycle

Maintain issuer identity, credential subject, evidence/provenance, schema, issuance date, expiry, status/revocation, verification method and audit history. Verify issuer authority and accreditation where a regulated claim is made. Clearly label platform-issued badges as platform recognition, not professional licensure.

## Standards to evaluate

W3C Verifiable Credentials, Open Badges and European Digital Credentials for Learning. A format alone does not prove that an issuer is authoritative.
