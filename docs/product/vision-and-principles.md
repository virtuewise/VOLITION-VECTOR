# Product vision and principles

## Vision

Make opportunity easier to discover, capability easier to demonstrate and trustworthy collaboration more accessible across borders and professional fields.

## Audience

A broad ecosystem for individuals, businesses, organizations, buyers, service providers, educators, mentors and credential issuers. The launch experience must welcome experienced professionals and people entering the economy for the first time.

## Principles

- Ecosystem, not a single-purpose job board.
- Balanced value for individuals, organizations and buyers.
- Evidence-based trust with transparent provenance.
- Fair access for economically disadvantaged users.
- Accessible by default; mobile-aware and low-bandwidth tolerant.
- Explainable recommendations and human accountability.
- No undisclosed pay-to-win ranking.
- Platform-issued recognition is distinct from external qualifications.
