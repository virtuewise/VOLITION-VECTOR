# Compliance and assurance control register

This is an engineering planning register, not legal advice, a certification or a declaration of compliance. Applicability depends on platform role, users, contracts, countries and actual processing.

| Area                            | Design work                                                                                                              | Evidence required                                                        |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| GDPR / UK GDPR                  | Lawful basis, transparency, minimization, rights, processor contracts, DPIA, transfers and retention                     | Records of processing, notices, DPIAs, DSAR tests and processor register |
| Digital Services Act            | Role mapping, trader traceability where applicable, notices/actions, transparency and appeals                            | Legal assessment, notice logs, trader workflow and reporting             |
| EU AI Act                       | AI inventory, role/risk classification, prohibited-practice review, transparency and high-risk controls where applicable | AI register, technical documentation, risk controls and human oversight  |
| Platform Work Directive         | Assess scope, algorithmic management and worker protections                                                              | Country transposition analysis and product impact assessment             |
| European Accessibility Act      | Determine covered services and exemptions; accessibility lifecycle                                                       | WCAG 2.2 AA testing and remediation records                              |
| Payments / PSD2 / local regimes | Provider and funds-flow assessment; avoid unauthorized custody                                                           | Legal opinion, provider contracts and PCI scope assessment               |
| Consumer law / VAT / DAC7       | Determine trader/platform role, reporting and tax obligations                                                            | Jurisdiction matrix, seller disclosures and tax workflows                |
| eIDAS / digital credentials     | Assess identity and trust-service claims                                                                                 | Issuer assurance, status and revocation records                          |

## Assurance roadmap

Consider ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO/IEC 27701:2025, ISO/IEC 42001 and SOC 2 Type II as appropriate. Each requires a scoped program and independent assessment; do not claim certification before it is earned. Use OWASP ASVS 5.0 as an application security verification baseline and WCAG 2.2 AA as the accessibility target.

## Jurisdiction launch gate

Before enabling a country, document entity/contracting model, platform role, employment/intermediary implications, consumer rules, tax, payments, privacy transfers, AI obligations, accessibility and dispute handling. Obtain qualified local legal advice.
