# Product and engineering decision log

- Central product identity: ecosystem.
- Initial audience: broad, with inclusive journeys for individuals and organizations.
- Competitive position: balanced value across participants, trust, intelligence and equitable access.
- Architecture: modular monolith first; see ADR 0001.
- Transactional source of truth: PostgreSQL.
- Payments: provider-neutral adapter; no direct custody assumption.
- AI: decision support first; consequential decisions require governance and human review.
- Release policy: stable, supported, exact dependency versions only.
