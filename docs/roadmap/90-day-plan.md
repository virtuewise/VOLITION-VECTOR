# 90-day delivery plan

This is a sequencing proposal, not a guarantee. Staffing, legal review, provider onboarding and testing determine the schedule. Security and compliance gates are not optional.

## Days 1–15 — Foundation

Confirm scope, personas, launch jurisdictions and operating model; resolve exact stable dependency matrix and lockfile; complete monorepo, CI, local infrastructure, design system and ADR process; define data classification, threat model, authorization and tenancy; commission privacy, platform-role, payments and AI legal analysis.

## Days 16–30 — Identity and workspace

Account lifecycle, authentication and recovery; individual and organization profiles, memberships and roles; audited admin/support workflows; API contracts, migrations, audit events and consent/notice foundations.

## Days 31–60 — Core opportunity workflows

Opportunity and service listings, search and discovery; applications/proposals, messaging and project workspace; trust evidence and moderation; provider sandbox and ledger tests; accessibility, localization and user research.

## Days 61–90 — Intelligence and controlled pilot

Explainable matching and user controls; credential issuer integrations; payment sandbox end-to-end, refunds and reconciliation; governance, appeals, analytics and equity measurement; penetration test, DPIA/legal gates, accessibility audit and pilot readiness review.

## Exit criteria

Pilot only when critical security findings are closed, data flows and legal roles reviewed, backup restoration demonstrated, support/incident runbooks exist, user research validates journeys and no unreviewed high-impact automated decision is enabled.
