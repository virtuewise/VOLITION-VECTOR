# Opportunity Equity

Build a genuine free access path for people with limited resources. Core discovery, profile creation, skill evidence and applying to suitable opportunities should not require premium payment.

## Fair discovery controls
- Do not sell undisclosed ranking dominance.
- Label sponsored placements and separate them from relevance ranking.
- Monitor exposure and outcomes for new entrants and underrepresented geographies without unlawfully inferring protected traits.
- Provide profile quality guidance and explainable match factors.
- Offer accessible learning, mentoring, portfolio support and sponsored credential pathways.
- Paid features are additive, not a gate to basic participation.

## Measurement
Track qualified opportunity exposure, response rates, conversion, time-to-first-opportunity, repeat outcomes, accessibility barriers and geographic coverage using privacy-preserving aggregation.
